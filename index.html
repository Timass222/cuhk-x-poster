Timestamps are a free sensor

What the session decoder changes, step by step. Real hidden-test clips; about 1 minute, no sound.

We recognise 40 daily activities from depth, infrared, skeleton and IMU clips, without RGB, on people never seen in training. Clip start times chain the clips into sessions, and a second-order decoder labels the whole session with no test labels.

How the decoder result is computed

For every clip, each of the 40 classes gets a score:

score(c) ∝ clip(c)0.85 × T(a, b → c)
T(a, b → c) ∝ (0.75·P2(c | a, b) + 0.25·P1(c | b))0.75
  • clip(c)probability from the five fused branches for this clip alone
  • P1, P2how often c followed the last one or the last two actions in the training lists
  • ^0.75with renormalising over the 40 classes, flattens the tables, so unseen transitions keep a small chance
  • sessionforward–backward runs this over the whole session, averaging over uncertain earlier clips and looking at the next clip too
clip power 0.85two-back weight 0.75flattening 0.75pseudo-counts 0.5 / 0.1
